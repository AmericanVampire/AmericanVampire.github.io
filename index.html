Realm Guard

A dedicated Windows workspace for your Minecraft Bedrock Realm. Monitor players, manage your Realm, and keep the records that matter in one place.

Windows 10 & 11 / Minecraft Bedrock Realms

No required add-ons
No resource packs or Realm modifications.
Local-first records
A private SQLite database on your PC.
Achievement friendly
Monitoring does not require cheats.

World commands require cheats enabled in the active world and sufficient in-game permissions. Enabling cheats disables achievements for that world. Realm Guard does not disable achievements on its own.

Monitoring

Follow your Realm's activity and keep player information organized, with searchable records stored on your PC.

Player records
Gamertags, Xbox XUIDs, permissions, last activity, pending invitations, banned members, labels, review flags, and private local notes.
Five log views
Server activity includes deaths, kills, and system events. Separate views cover joins and departures with local timestamps, observed chat, command results, and moderation history. Search and filter by the information you need. Command logs identify the sending account and Minecraft's completion, failure, or no-response result.
Live Monitor
Inspect connection state, session start, Bedrock protocol version, reconnection attempts, packet counts, observed events, and disconnect details when troubleshooting.

Administration

Manage membership, review moderation history, and access authorized Realm tools from a dedicated console.

Commands will not execute while cheats are disabled. World commands require cheats enabled in the Realm's active world and sufficient in-game permissions. Enabling cheats disables achievements for that world. Realm Guard cannot bypass these requirements.

Members & moderation
Invite by Xbox XUID, change permissions, remove or ban members, clear bans, and disconnect online players with an optional reason. Keep review queues, labels, notes, and action history together.
Realm & backups
Change the Realm name and message, refresh its current state, select among three world slots, and review game mode, difficulty, cheats, PvP, command blocks, forced game mode, required packs, and spawn protection. Download available backups. Choose a preferred folder, track progress, cancel downloads, and review saved archives.
Command console
Browse slash commands with syntax, descriptions, contextual suggestions, online-player targets, and local history. Quick commands cover teleporting, game modes, time, and weather.

Owner tools such as backups, invitations, and membership management do not require cheats; they require an authorized Realm owner account.

Discord & Exports

Keep your community informed and turn saved Realm activity into records you can use outside the app.

One live Discord embed
Authorize Discord and choose a server and channel. Publish the Realm name, active world-slot name, online players, player count, and update time in one maintained message.
CSV & plain text
Export saved logs as spreadsheet-ready CSV or readable text, with optional date ranges, a preferred export folder, and local export history. Private administrator notes are excluded from moderation reports.

What you need

Windows & Realm access
A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC, an active Minecraft Bedrock Realm, a Microsoft account with access to it, internet access, and an active Realm Guard Microsoft Store subscription. Realm players can join from any supported Bedrock platform.
Observer account
Realm Guard connects through authorized Microsoft and Xbox services. A dedicated Minecraft observer account is recommended for continuous monitoring.
Owner account
An optional authorized Realm owner account unlocks owner-only management features. Realm Guard respects the account's permissions and the world's settings.
Windows integration
Launch with Windows, open normally or to the notification area, minimize to tray, and reconnect the observer automatically.

Privacy

Your Realm records stay on your PC. Realm Guard contains no advertising, third-party analytics, publisher telemetry, or cloud synchronization.

Private local storage
Realm records are stored locally in a private SQLite database on your PC. Player details, labels, administrator notes, logs, moderation and command history, exports, archives, and settings remain in private application storage.
Microsoft & Xbox
Microsoft and Xbox process authentication, Realm access, and Microsoft Store licensing.
Optional Discord publishing
Discord receives only the live Realm information sent to the server and channel you explicitly authorize.
