The Realm Guard Platform

A command center for realm owners, admins, and community builders.

Realm Guard is being built as a practical companion for Minecraft Bedrock administration: a place to bring together player information, server activity, logs, and realm details.

Instead of scattering important details between chats, screenshots, and text files, Realm Guard gives the server owner a single app-centered workflow for managing what matters.

What It Helps With
  • Track members and realm activity in one place.
  • Keep player and realm information ready for review.
  • Keep admin-only details separated from general realm records.
  • Organize server operations around repeatable admin workflows.
Core Capabilities

Everything important about the realm, shaped into one clean workspace.
