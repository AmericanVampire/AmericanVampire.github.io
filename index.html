The Admin Console for Minecraft Bedrock Realms

Monitor players, review activity, manage members, run commands, download Realm backups, and maintain a live Discord roster from one dedicated Windows application.

Realm Guard gives Realm owners and administrators a dedicated control center for operating a Minecraft Bedrock Realm. It replaces scattered screenshots, chat history, handwritten notes, and separate tools with one organized administration workflow.

Realm Guard connects through authorized Microsoft and Xbox services and observes the Realm through a dedicated Minecraft account. An optional Realm owner account unlocks owner-level management features.
