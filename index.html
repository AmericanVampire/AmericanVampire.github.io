The Realm Guard Platform

A command center for realm owners, admins, and community builders.

Realm Guard is being built as a practical companion for Minecraft Bedrock administration: a place to bring together player information, server activity, public statistics, and realm-facing website content.

Instead of scattering important details between chats, screenshots, text files, and manual website edits, Realm Guard gives the server owner a single app-centered workflow for managing what matters and publishing what the community should see.

What It Helps With
  • Track members and realm activity in one place.
  • Prepare leaderboard and player information for public pages.
  • Keep admin-only details separate from community-facing data.
  • Organize server operations around repeatable workflows.
