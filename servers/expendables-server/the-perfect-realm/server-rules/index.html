The Perfect Realm

Server Rules

These rules apply to every player using The Perfect Realm. All members are expected to review and follow the current server rules.

01

Respect

We are a professional and mature server and expect everyone to uphold that standard. Childish behavior and foul language are not accepted. This includes unlawful, harmful, threatening, abusive, harassing, defamatory, vulgar, obscene, hateful, homophobic, sexually explicit, racial, ethnic, or otherwise offensive language or conduct. These standards also apply to player names, gamertags, and in-game named items. Be respectful.

02

General Rules

Use common sense. When cutting down a tree, remove the entire trunk so the leaves can despawn. Do not build 1×1 towers, lava walls, water walls, unrealistic floating objects, or unsupported builds. Lag machines are prohibited, and any machine that causes lag during extended operation must be turned off. Trees removed from inner plots must be replanted.

03

Theft

Theft will not be tolerated. Stealing from another player may result in account suspension, a ban, or up to one week of in-game jail time.

04

Griefing & Spamming

Do not destroy anything that is not your own. This includes mob griefing and every other form of griefing. Do not spam-build carpets, saplings, candles, blocks, or other objects anywhere in the realm.

05

VIP Plots & Player Property

VIP members may claim one secure plot on the mainland. Player-owned property must not be entered without permission; this applies to everyone. Use the sidewalks. Plot Lock is available, and each plot owner is responsible for ensuring it is enabled. Players who build away from the mainland must build outside the mainland map.

06

Boss Fights

The End dimension contains the designated Wither arena and safe zone. This is the only location where the Wither may be spawned. Spawning the Wither anywhere else may result in account suspension or a ban.

07

Restricted Areas

Some areas of the realm are restricted, including Demon's Plot of Remembrance (Plot 9). Any location marked or designated as restricted is not open to the public. Automated systems may take action when a player enters one of these areas.

08

Cheating & Exploits

Cheating and exploiting bugs are prohibited and may result in account suspension or a ban. This includes duplication, Castle Marketplace manipulation, exploitation of the Hellions Banking System, and similar abuse of server systems.
